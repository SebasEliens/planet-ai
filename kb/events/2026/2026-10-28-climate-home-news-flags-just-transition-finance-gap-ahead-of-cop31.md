Ahead of COP31 in Antalya, Climate Home News reports that funding meant to help workers and communities through the clean energy transition remains a small, poorly tracked share of climate finance, dominated by loans rather than grants for Just Energy Transition Partnerships (JETPs)[^s1]. It cites a new discussion paper from the Just Transition Finance Lab at the Grantham Research Institute, co-authored by Jodi-Ann Wang, which finds there is still no common tracker or taxonomy for just transition finance, calling the opacity a barrier to understanding flows[^s1]. The article also details a $20-million Global South Just Transition Fund launched in 2023 by ClimateWorks Foundation with IKEA and Laudes foundations, which has funded 60 community organisations, unions and researchers across JETP countries[^s1]. Climate Action Network International is pushing for the new Just Transition Mechanism, to be defined at COP31 negotiations, to include a 'resource mobilisation group' aimed at improving the quantity, quality and governance of just transition finance[^s1].
