On September 10, 2026, California Governor Gavin Newsom signed 12 bills framed as child online safety measures, including AB 1709, which EFF describes as a functional ban on social media use for people under 16[^eff-digital-literacy-bills-2026]. Newsom also signed AB 2071 and AB 2298, which EFF supported because they require schools to teach digital wellness, cybersecurity, and critical evaluation of AI-generated content instead of restricting access[^eff-digital-literacy-bills-2026]. EFF notes that AB 2071 was co-authored by students seeking better digital literacy resources, and argues these education-based laws are a constitutional alternative to bans that risk violating young people's First Amendment rights[^eff-digital-literacy-bills-2026].
