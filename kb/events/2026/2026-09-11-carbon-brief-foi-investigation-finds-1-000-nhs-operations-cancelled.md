Carbon Brief submitted FOI requests to all 140 NHS trusts and health boards with surgical theatres in England and Wales to quantify heat-related disruption during the record May and June 2026 heatwaves.[^carbonbrief-nhs-heatwave-2026] Of the 59 trusts that responded with data, at least 1,110 operations were cancelled over a 17-day period spanning the heatwave alerts, including 167 orthopaedic procedures, eight heart operations, seven skin-cancer removals and 10 emergency surgeries; another 18 procedures were abandoned mid-way through.[^carbonbrief-nhs-heatwave-2026] Clinicians attributed most cancellations to ventilation and air-conditioning systems failing above certain temperatures, raising infection risks and threatening patient and staff safety, with Surrey and Sussex Healthcare NHS Trust reporting the highest number of cancellations.[^carbonbrief-nhs-heatwave-2026] Because fewer than half of trusts track or reported this data, Carbon Brief concludes the real scale of heat-driven surgical disruption on the NHS is likely far greater than the figures show.[^carbonbrief-nhs-heatwave-2026]
