EFF published a recap of California's 2026 legislative session, noting it tracked nearly 100 bills covering AI, surveillance, and privacy issues over the two-year session[^s1]. Among the wins, Governor Newsom signed SB 947 ('No Robo Bosses'), which restricts employers from using automated decision-making alone to discipline or fire workers, and AB 1883, which bars workplace surveillance tools from inferring emotions or collecting neural data[^s1]. Other signed bills included SB 747, enabling damages lawsuits against federal officials for constitutional violations, and AB 1854/AB 1930, which strengthen shield laws protecting reproductive and gender-affirming healthcare records from out-of-state legal demands[^s1]. EFF also noted it helped block an expansion of the state's age-gating law to browsers via amendments to AB 1856, while mourning the failure of AB 1337, a bill it co-sponsored to update California's Information Practices Act[^s1].
