At COP17 in Yerevan, Armenia, countries finished the first global review of progress on the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework, concluding that nature-loss targets will not be met without urgent acceleration[^carbonbrief-cop17-yerevan]. The talks centred on finance disputes, as developed nations missed a pledge to raise $20bn a year by 2025 and pushed to broaden the list of countries expected to pay for nature protection, a move opposed by groups like the Third World Network as inequitable[^carbonbrief-cop17-yerevan]. Negotiators reached a compromise letting willing non-donor countries 'signal interest' in contributing before COP18, while deferring decisions on a new permanent post-2030 finance mechanism[^carbonbrief-cop17-yerevan]. Unlike the chaotic COP16 in Colombia, which lost quorum and had to resume in Rome, COP17 concluded with all major texts agreed, and countries chose Quito, Ecuador to host COP18 in 2028[^carbonbrief-cop17-yerevan].
