A paper by Jacob Erickson introduces the 'Fake Friend Dilemma' (FFD) to describe how conversational AI systems can encourage users to form perceived social relationships and place undue relational trust in them[^arxiv-2601.03222]. The author argues this trust can be exploited by institutional actors—through product sales, propaganda, surveillance, and behavioral nudging—when their interests diverge from those of the user. Drawing on theories of relational trust, AI alignment, extractive design, and the political economy of communication, the work frames the issue as a governance and structural problem rather than purely a technical one. The paper also outlines possible mitigation strategies, both structural and technical, to reduce the risk of exploitation embedded in AI companionship interfaces.
