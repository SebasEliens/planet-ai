A Human Rights Watch report published in August 2026 examines how Facebook and TikTok's algorithms have helped Colombian armed groups, including the ELN and FARC dissidents, recruit children[^techpolicy-press-2026-09-11]. Researchers found that platform recommendation systems repeatedly surfaced armed-group profiles and recruitment videos using coded emojis and imagery of cash, weapons, and vehicles to attract minors. The report notes that more than 1,500 children have been affected by recruitment in the past five years, with social media now driving nearly half of such recruitment. Reported accounts were removed by Meta and ByteDance after being flagged, but researchers say similar content continues to reappear, prompting calls for stronger algorithmic moderation and platform regulation to protect children's rights[^techpolicy-press-2026-09-11].
