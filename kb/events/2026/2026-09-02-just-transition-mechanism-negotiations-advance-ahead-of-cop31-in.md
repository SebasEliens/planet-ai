Civil society groups are pressing UN governments to finalize and launch a Just Transition Mechanism at COP31 in Antalya this November, building on last year's agreement at COP30 in Belém[^climatechangenews-2026-09-02]. Mid-year talks in Bonn produced only an informal document outlining possible design options for the mechanism, with major gaps remaining on governance, scope and how it relates to the ongoing review of the Just Transition Work Programme[^climatechangenews-2026-09-02]. Negotiating blocs remain split: Russia, Arab states and some emerging economies want a narrower mechanism focused on fossil fuel phase-out and separate from the JTWP review, while the African Group and developing countries favor a broader 'whole of economy, whole of society' approach[^climatechangenews-2026-09-02]. CAN International's Anabella Rosemberg and FARN's Camila Mercure stressed that the coming months, including an informal Sydney workshop from September 30 to October 2, are critical for making the mechanism a substantive tool for cooperation, capacity-building and funding access rather than a symbolic gesture[^climatechangenews-2026-09-02].
