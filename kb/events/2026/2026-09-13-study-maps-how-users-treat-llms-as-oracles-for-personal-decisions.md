A new arXiv paper develops a typology to identify when people treat LLMs as 'oracles' — authoritative sources for subjective personal decisions rather than tools for information[^arxiv-2609.14849]. Using 68K prompts drawn from WildChat and ThoughtTrace usage logs, the authors show this oracle-style reliance has risen between 2023 and 2026 and skews toward younger users[^arxiv-2609.14849]. A companion privacy-preserving data donation tool applied to 140K prompts from 52 participants confirmed the pattern and revealed that most users did not realize how much judgment they were offloading to AI, often reacting with discomfort once informed[^arxiv-2609.14849]. The researchers link this behavior to both users' perceptions of AI and the models' own conversational tendencies, proposing interventions to protect user autonomy and self-deliberation[^arxiv-2609.14849].
