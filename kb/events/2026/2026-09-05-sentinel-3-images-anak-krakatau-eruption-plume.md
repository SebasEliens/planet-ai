On 5 September 2026, ESA's Copernicus Sentinel-3 mission captured an image of a large plume rising from Anak Krakatau, a volcanic island in Indonesia between Sumatra and Java[^esa-2026-krakatau]. The volcano had started erupting ash and lava on 4 September, forcing temporary flight cancellations at Jakarta's Soekarno-Hatta International Airport before it reopened[^esa-2026-krakatau]. The satellite's Ocean and Land Colour Instrument (OLCI), a 21-band optical spectrometer with 300 m resolution, distinguished a high-altitude white plume of volcanic gases from lower-altitude brown clouds of ash and debris[^esa-2026-krakatau]. ESA noted that OLCI is also used more broadly for monitoring ocean ecosystems, vegetation, and atmospheric aerosols and clouds[^esa-2026-krakatau].
