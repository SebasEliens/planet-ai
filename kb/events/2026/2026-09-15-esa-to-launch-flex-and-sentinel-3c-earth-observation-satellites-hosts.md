ESA announced that its FLEX Earth Explorer mission and the Copernicus Sentinel-3C satellite will launch together on a Vega-C rocket from Europe's Spaceport in French Guiana in the early hours of 15 September 2026[^esa-press-45-2026]. FLEX is designed to be the first satellite to measure plant fluorescence from space, offering new data on photosynthetic activity, ecosystem productivity and vegetation response to climate stress[^esa-press-45-2026]. Sentinel-3C, the third satellite in the Sentinel-3 series, will extend long-term monitoring of oceans, land, ice and atmosphere for climate and environmental applications under the EU Copernicus programme[^esa-press-45-2026]. Following the night launch, ESA, the European Commission and EUMETSAT hosted a media and VIP re-watch event at ESA's Space Operations Centre (ESOC) in Darmstadt, including mission briefings and control-room visits[^esa-press-45-2026].
