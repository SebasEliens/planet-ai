Carbon Brief's analysis draws on European Commission, EU Joint Research Centre, ECIU and national statistics to show that 2026's extreme heat, drought and wildfires sharply reduced European crop output.[^carbonbrief-2026-crops] Most EU countries are projected to see lower cereal production and yields in 2026 versus 2025, with France alone expecting an almost 8 megatonne drop in cereal output and a 35% fall in maize production to a four-decade low.[^carbonbrief-2026-crops] ECIU estimated that June's record-breaking heatwave, found by attribution scientists to have been 'virtually impossible' 50 years ago, contributed to roughly €2-2.3bn in cumulative grain revenue losses concentrated in France, Germany, Hungary and Spain.[^carbonbrief-2026-crops] The UK is on track for one of its five worst cereal and oilseed harvests since 1984, and Triodos Bank estimated the wider extreme weather could shave around 1% (about €180bn) off EU GDP this year.[^carbonbrief-2026-crops]
