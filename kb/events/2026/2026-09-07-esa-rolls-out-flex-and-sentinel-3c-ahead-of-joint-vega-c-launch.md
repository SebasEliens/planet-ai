ESA reported that its FLEX satellite and the Copernicus Sentinel-3C satellite, encased within a Vega-C rocket fairing, were rolled out to the launch tower at Europe's Spaceport in French Guiana on 7 September 2026[^esa-flex-sentinel3c-launchtower]. The two satellites are set for a joint launch on Vega-C flight VV30, scheduled for 15 September at 03:21 CEST[^esa-flex-sentinel3c-launchtower]. FLEX carries a Fluorescence Imaging Spectrometer built to detect faint fluorescence emitted by plants during photosynthesis, offering insight into vegetation health and stress responses[^esa-flex-sentinel3c-launchtower]. Sentinel-3C, the third satellite in the Copernicus Sentinel-3 series, will continue systematic monitoring of oceans, land, ice and atmosphere, supporting near-real-time forecasting[^esa-flex-sentinel3c-launchtower].
