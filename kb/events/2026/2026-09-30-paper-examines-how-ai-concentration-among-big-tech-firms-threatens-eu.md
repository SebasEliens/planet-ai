A paper posted to arXiv on 30 September 2026 by Marcin Marciniak argues that advanced AI development is increasingly concentrated among a small group of vertically integrated tech firms that control infrastructure, data, models and distribution channels[^arxiv-2609.39285]. The author contends this concentration functions as a form of private governance over conditions under which fundamental rights are exercised in the EU, affecting access, privacy, scientific freedom, labour conditions, and environmental protection[^arxiv-2609.39285]. The analysis draws on evidence from data-centre expansion in Ireland, the UK, US, and Chile, and on studies of automation and collective bargaining, showing benefits can be broadly distributed while power and returns stay concentrated[^arxiv-2609.39285]. The paper recommends combining competition policy with rights-based impact assessments, interoperability mandates, public research capacity, worker participation, and enforceable remedy mechanisms[^arxiv-2609.39285].
