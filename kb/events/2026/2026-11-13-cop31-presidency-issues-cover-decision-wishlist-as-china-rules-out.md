At COP31 in Antalya, the presidency published a summary of countries' views as a basis for a possible cover decision, covering ideas such as managing the overshoot of 1.5C warming, a 35% electrification target by 2035, and milestones for tripling adaptation finance[^src1]. The presidency explicitly said the note "is not intended to reflect consensus, nor the strength or prevalence of views expressed"[^src1]. Some emerging economies, along with Saudi Arabia, questioned both the need for a cover decision and the scientific basis of a UNEP overshoot report, calling it unreviewed "grey literature"[^src1]. Separately, reporting indicated China has withdrawn from the running to host COP33 in 2028, a pivotal year for the next global climate progress review, leaving Uzbekistan, South Korea, Indonesia and Thailand as possible Asia-Pacific hosts[^src1].
