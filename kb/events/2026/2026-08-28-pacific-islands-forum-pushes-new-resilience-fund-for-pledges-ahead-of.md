Pacific Islands Forum leaders, including Australia and New Zealand, are set to launch a formal call for pledges to the Pacific Resilience Facility (PRF) at a high-level meeting in Palau ahead of COP31[^src1]. The PRF, formally launched in May 2026, is designed to bypass slow global funding channels and give communities direct, simplified access to climate finance, according to Fiji's assistant minister for foreign affairs Lenora Qereqeretabua[^src1]. The fund has so far raised about $172 million, with roughly $67 million from Australia, and is aiming to reach $500 million by year end[^src1]. Advocates from groups like the Pacific Islands Climate Action Network say the facility's success will depend on whether disbursement processes remain simple enough for communities to actually access the money[^src1].
