NASA and IBM published Prithvi, a foundation model pre-trained on Harmonized Landsat
Sentinel-2 (HLS) satellite imagery, as an open release on Hugging Face.[^nasa-release]
The release included model weights and fine-tuning examples for tasks such as flood
mapping, burn-scar detection, and land-use classification.[^hf-model] NASA framed the
model as part of its open-science effort to make Earth-observation data more usable by
researchers without large compute budgets.[^nasa-release]
