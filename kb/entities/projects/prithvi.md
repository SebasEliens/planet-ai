Prithvi is a family of foundation models for Earth-observation and weather/climate data,
developed by [NASA](../orgs/nasa.md) and [IBM](../orgs/ibm.md) and released openly on
Hugging Face. The models are pre-trained on satellite imagery and reanalysis data and
fine-tuned for downstream tasks such as flood mapping and land-use classification.
